AV FAQs

What are your rates?

It depends on you! Our quotes are completely custom and tailored to you and your event. Get in contact and we would be happy to create a free, no obligation quote to help get your event started!
If you'd rather view some pre made packages, visit our "Packages" page for some options.

Do you offer setup?

We are more than happy to provide a full comprehensive setup and pack down of all the gear letting you sit back and relax or crack on creating another aspect of your event.

What areas do you service?

We are based in The Isle of Purbeck, South Dorset but get in touch for a quote and we will see if we can service you!

What equipment do you offer?

We have a comprehensive store of equipment ready to go to help create your event. Need something specialised that we don't have? No worries! We have partnerships with many other companies and will make sure that we have everything we need for your perfect event.
