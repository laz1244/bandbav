Who am i?

I am a live event technician in the south of England running my own business BandB AV.
Through this business i've experienced lots of different types of shows and built experience and knowledge some people my age would love to have. Having already done shows for big names like Toyah Wilcox i have managed to cement my love and passion for the industry already.

Having now been in the industry for a decade i have been able to get hands on experience within the industry and develop my skill set starting off in small venues like churches packing up and down PA systems weekly i quickly developed and understanding and love for the industry and learnt many key skills that would help me over the past 10 years. 

Having started to purchase my own equipment i joined a good friend and started my next step into the the world of live events and started my own business helping me gain more exposure to what the working world within the event industry is like and diversify my skills learning about different parts of the job and diversifying my skillset from just sound to cover a wide range of areas including lighting and projection.
